## 1. Identity: names, titles, faction, attribute, profile data

### Takeaway
Her full name is Remielle Dan (VI: "Remielle Dan"; CN 蕾米埃尔·丹; JP レミエール・ダン). She is a first-generation Void Hunter (VI "Thợ Săn Lỗ Hổng đời đầu"; CN 初代虚狩) and a former Lieutenant Colonel of the Guard of Staves (CN 节杖军中校; VI in-game "Quyền Trượng Quân"). She is currently a member of the Covenant of Dayat (VI "Hội Tri Thức"; CN 达识结社; JP ダアト結社). Gameplay data: S-Rank, Lumiflux (VI "Lưu Minh"), Anomaly (VI "Dị Thường"), born 14 March, 167 cm, of the winged Thiren race. She became playable in 3.1, released 2026-07-29.

## 3. Backstory: chronology of her life (pre-story)

### Takeaway
Her life runs in this order:
1. Child prodigy and dancer, childhood friend of Beliora Vike.
2. Joined the Guard of Staves young and became Lieutenant Colonel.
3. Inherited Thaumiel.
4. Pushed back the Dark Wall 37 km with Colonel Vike and the Falkenhayn leader, and became one of the 7 first Void Hunters.
5. Joined Project Sunseeker (CN 逐日计划 / VI Kế Hoạch Trục Nhật), run by Arche and the original Sunbringer, during the "Day of Silence" (CN 静默之日 / VI Ngày Tĩnh Lặng).
6. Vike became an Enchainer and Remielle killed her.
7. Remielle's own partial transformation put her into roughly 100 years of temporal stasis.
8. She woke in Pursenas Hollow about a year before Phaethon reached Roscaelifer, and sealed Thaumiel away.
9. She joined the Covenant of Dayat.
10. She confronted Eldreda Sunbringer in Roscaelifer and was declared a wanted criminal (the so-called "attempted assassination").
