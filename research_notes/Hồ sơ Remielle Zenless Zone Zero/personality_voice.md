## Personality (official)

### Takeaway
Officially she is sweet, smiling, composed and overly familiar. She closes distance quickly with jokes but keeps a careful buffer, is elusive, appears at key moments to steer events, and teases the Proxy more than anyone. Underneath she hides pain: her Enchainer transformation, which she refuses to let anyone see, and grief for a lost era.

### Cited Findings
- Official profile ("详细情报 / Chi Tiết / 詳細"): she always wears a sweet, unhurried smile and stays calm in any crisis. She is even over-familiar (CN 自来熟; VI "nhiệt tình quá mức"; JA 馴れ馴れしい), so people find it hard to stay on guard. She is good at closing distance with half-joking words and actions, but always keeps "just the right distance", which makes her hard to read — [HoYoWiki-CN/VI/JA](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112)
- She comes and goes without a trace, yet always turns up at unexpected critical moments and guides the situation — [HoYoWiki-CN/VI/JA](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112)
- Official note: she enjoys teasing people with harmless jokes, and the Proxy clearly gets "special treatment" (CN 「优待」; VI "đãi ngộ đặc biệt"; JA 「特別扱い」) — [HoYoWiki-CN/VI/JA](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112)
- Background: she was gifted from childhood and mastered most fields quickly. She joined the Guard of Staves young, was promoted out of turn to lieutenant colonel, and inherited the Void Hunter weapon Thaumiel. Together with Colonel Vike and the leader of the Falkenhayn mercenaries she drove back the Dark Wall and became a Void Hunter — [HoYoWiki-CN/VI/JA](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112)
- Hidden pain (unlocked at a higher trust level): her Enchainer transformation has not stopped, and overusing the Void Hunter weapon speeds it up. At a critical point frightening symptoms appear on her skin, and she seems extremely averse to letting anyone see her like that — [HoYoWiki-CN/VI/JA](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112); [BWIKI](https://wiki.biligame.com/zzz/%E8%95%BE%E7%B1%B3%E5%9F%83%E5%B0%94)
- Proxy footnote: Belle (CN 铃) says Remielle smiles a lot but doesn't seem as happy as she looks, and asks how to help. Wise (CN 哲) replies that she is harder to approach than she seems, but when she needs it, her "accomplice" will be by her side — [HoYoWiki-CN/VI/JA](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112)
- Sense of duty under the playfulness: in Mindscape 3 she answers a warning by saying she knows, but someone has to do these things — [HoYoWiki-CN/VI/JA, Mindscape 3 "时间分岔的花园 / Khu Vườn Rẽ Nhánh Thời Gian / あまたの時を分かつ庭"](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112)
- Self-sacrifice and pushing the Proxy away: the official 3.1 key-art lines (JA gallery, "Ver.3.1「ロング・グッドバイ」") have her say the Proxy almost made her forget she no longer belongs to this era, that the Proxy must not come yet, and that she will go on alone from here — [HoYoWiki-JA gallery](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112)
- Joking vs. lying: the moonlit outfit text has her admit that her jokes and lies are hard to tell apart, then propose another round of a question game — [HoYoWiki-CN/VI/JA gallery "月夜密语 / Thì Thầm Dưới Trăng / 月夜のささやき"](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112)

### Inferences
- Roleplay core: a teasing, flirty, calm surface with real melancholy and loneliness beneath. She deflects serious questions with jokes or games, and won't show weakness or her erosion. Her "accomplice" framing makes the Proxy a co-conspirator she trusts more than she admits.
- The reported "stoic/blunt facade" is not what the official profile says. The official tone is sweet and playful, not blunt.

- Agent-gift and like/dislike data: not found.

## Speech style and voice lines (original-language priority: VI > CN > JA)

### Takeaway
In every language she speaks casually and warmly to the Proxy, with a teasing lilt.
- **CN**: she calls herself 我 and the Proxy 你 / 共犯 / 亲爱的共犯, with soft particles 呢/吧/么/哦 and a trailing "~".
- **JA**: she calls herself 私 and the Proxy キミ (katakana) / 共犯者さん / 親愛なる共犯者さん. Endings are casual and girlish: 〜だもんね？, 〜ちゃう, 〜かな？, 〜よ, 〜ってば, 〜だね, with ねえ as an opener.
- **VI**: she calls herself "tôi" and the Proxy "cô/cậu" (gender-variable) / "đồng phạm (thân mến)", with particles nhỉ~, nhé, đấy, chứ, à, and interjections "Này", "Haiz".

### Cited Findings
- **Signature line** (the page epigraph, attributed to Remielle; the same line as the pixiv quote): she muses that "accomplices" makes her heart race more than "dance partners".
  - VI: says "tôi", calls them "đồng phạm" vs "Bạn nhảy", ends with "nhỉ~".
  - JA: 『共犯者』 vs 『ダンスパートナー』, ending 〜だもんね？ … ドキドキしちゃう.
  - [HoYoWiki-VI/JA desc](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112); [pixiv](https://dic.pixiv.net/en/a/Remielle%20Dan)
- **Mindscape lines** (module 意象影画 / Phim Ý Cảnh / 心象映画). Each ends with a line of hers:
  - M1 "青涩誓言 / Lời Thề Non Nớt / 無垢なる誓い": as a recruit she grumbles to "Beli" (CN 贝莉, VI "Beli", JA ベリ) that the oath is too long. In JA she speaks casually (ねぇベリ…〜のかな…？) while Beli answers politely (〜ですよ). Beli teases that longer ones are coming.
  - M2 "倾落喧嚣 / Huyên Náo Lắng Xuống / 喧噪に傾倒": with mock reluctance she offers to dance with someone who has no partner at the end of the ball. CN uses "唉，我就勉为其难…"; VI "Haiz, tôi đành miễn cưỡng…"; JA "しょうがない。特別に一曲だけ、付き合ってあげるよ".

## Relationships (through v3.3)

### Takeaway
Confirmed ties:
- Her old Void Hunter comrades and superiors: Colonel Vike, the Falkenhayn mercenary leader, and "Beli".
- The architects of Project Sunchaser: Arche and Sunbringer. Sunbringer is now an ambiguous antagonist or foil.
- Her current faction: Dashi Society / Hội Tri Thức, plus the Bangboo Ariel.
- The En-Nah gang, who hid her.
- Cissia, who is hostile and jealous.
- The Proxy, her "accomplice" and special teasing target.

### Cited Findings
- **Colonel Vike** (CN 维克上校, JA ヴァイク大佐, VI "thượng úy Vike"): a fellow Void Hunter. Officially both died on the Day of Silence. In truth both joined Project Sunchaser, which failed and "lost" both Void Hunters. Thaumiel kept Remielle from fully becoming an Enchainer, and she slept for 100+ years — [HoYoWiki-CN/VI/JA](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/zzz/entry/1112)
